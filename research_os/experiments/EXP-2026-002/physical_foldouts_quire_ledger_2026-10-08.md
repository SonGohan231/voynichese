## Non-unique ordering — hard vs candidate constraints

- **HARD 1**: Physical paper/parchment conjoined units are indivisible. Foldout page/panel IDs are not standalone bifolia; q9/q14/q15/q17/q19 geometries require custom image-validated models.
- **HARD 2**: Current visible quire/fold structure is recorded as O1 material placement. A later gathering mark is not an independently justified original-order edge.
- **HARD 3**: Missing material occupies explicit placeholder nodes. A plausible location inside the current collation is not recovered original text or verified original stacking.
- **CANDIDATE q9**: Alternative axis can be considered only as a rival physical orientation; no definitive inference about old sewing is possible without verifying the cuts/fold.
- **CANDIDATE q13**: Central f78/f81 hypothesis is visual-layout motivated; record it as a branch of the constraint family, never silently impose it.
- **CANDIDATE Singulion stacks**: Within-quire permutation of intact physical sheets is merely a deliberately restricted geometric control, not proof that historical sheets were grouped by today's quires.
- **OPEN original sheet sequence**: Especially herbal quires 1–8, and q13/q20 only text-proposed sequences known to us; no full, physically mandated ancient order has been obtained.
