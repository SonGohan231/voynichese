## Fixed stars

Across 26 years general precession is only about 0.363°. Fixed-star patterns therefore do not create a unique year-specific map. The informative phenomena are conjunctions, occultations, eclipses and the changing positions of planets against the fixed-star background.

## Interpretation boundary

A temporal match does not identify the manuscript date. A viable manuscript model must predict a page-specific feature—especially on Scorpio f73r—without choosing the feature after seeing the 1425 sky.
